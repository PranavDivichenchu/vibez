Our story

Juniper & Rye started as a Saturday table at the farmers' market: four loaves, one oven, and a rye starter named Juniper that is older than the business.

Today we bake six days a week for the neighbourhood, still from the same starter, still by hand.

How we got here

2016First market stall. Sold out by 9am.
2018Moved into the old print shop on Alder Street.
2021Added pastries, and a second oven to bake them in.
2024Started the monthly newsletter and the pickup menu.
